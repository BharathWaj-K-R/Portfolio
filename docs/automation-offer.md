# Productized automation offer

> I will remove one repetitive workflow from your business in 7–14 days. Fixed price. You approve the final system before it goes live.

## What is being sold

One clearly bounded workflow, removed from the client's manual process.

Examples:
- lead intake and triage
- contact-form routing
- appointment request handling
- customer onboarding
- repetitive reporting
- internal handoff and follow-up

## Validation rule

Do not invent savings, ROI, testimonials, or baseline times.

Every case study records:
1. the existing manual steps;
2. the measured manual duration;
3. the automated duration;
4. the exact timestamp of the automated run;
5. what changed;
6. what still requires human approval.

## Delivery constraint

The system is not considered complete until:
- the client can exercise the workflow end to end;
- failure paths are tested;
- secrets stay server-side;
- the client approves the production behavior;
- the workflow has at least one reusable asset extracted for the template library.

## Outreach constraint

One researched friction point.
One 90-second demonstration.
One message.
One human-approved send.
No follow-up sequence.
